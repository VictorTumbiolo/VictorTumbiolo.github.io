What it is

A first-person take on Pac-Man built in Unity. You play as Pac-Man, walking through a 3D maze with W A S D and mouse look, collecting coins while four ghosts chase you.

The maze and its waypoint graph are authored in the Unity editor. A set of C# scripts supplies everything else: player movement, ghost AI, collectibles and the game flow (lives, win and lose, resets). Each ghost precomputes shortest paths over the graph with Dijkstra's algorithm, then picks targets according to its own personality.

In the maze

How you move

W A S D

Walk

W A S D move you forward, left, back and right.

Look

The mouse turns the camera. Looking up and down is clamped to ±90°, so you can't flip over.

  • Movement is relative to where you're looking. The camera's forward and right directions are flattened onto the ground, so looking at the sky doesn't slow you down.
  • The player is a Rigidbody moved with MovePosition every physics step, with mouse look handled each frame.
  • Movement and look both freeze while a menu is on screen.

The ghosts

All four use the same pathfinding. What differs is the spot each one decides to run to. In first person, "ahead of Pac-Man" means wherever you're looking.

  • Blinky Red · the direct chaser

    Routes to the waypoint nearest you. If no waypoint is closer to you than he is, he skips the graph and heads straight at you.

  • Pinky Pink · the ambusher

    Aims about 4 tiles (12 units) ahead of where you're looking and routes to the nearest waypoint to that spot.

  • Inky Cyan · the flanker

    Takes the point 2 tiles (6 units) ahead of you, draws a line from Blinky to it, then doubles it. Where Inky goes depends on where Blinky is, just like the arcade original.

  • Clyde Orange · chases, then loses nerve

    Chases like Blinky until he gets within 32 units of you, then retreats to his home corner.

Power pellets

Eating a power pellet turns all four ghosts blue for 6 seconds. They slow from speed 5 to 3, turn around, and wander randomly (never straight back the way they came). Touching an afraid ghost eats it: it goes back to the ghost house for 3 seconds, then re-enters the maze. Touching a ghost that isn't afraid costs you a life.

How they find you

  1. A hand-built graph. Every waypoint in the maze lists its neighbors, set by hand in the Unity Inspector.
  2. Precomputed routes. When the game starts, each ghost runs Dijkstra's algorithm from every waypoint to every other and keeps only the first step of each shortest path in a lookup table.
  3. Cheap decisions. At each waypoint the ghost picks a destination, looks up the next hop, and walks there. All the heavy work was done up front, so per-frame cost is tiny.
  4. Tunnels count. The two tunnel ends are linked with zero-cost edges, so ghost routes can use the tunnel.

Game flow

  1. Home screen. The game starts frozen with the cursor free. Press play to hide the menu and unfreeze Pac-Man.
  2. Staggered ghosts. Blinky leaves at once and Pinky follows after 3 seconds. Inky and Clyde are held back until 30 and 60 coins.
  3. Getting caught. You lose one of your 3 lives and the round resets: positions, waypoints and ghosts all go back to the start, behind a Resume button. Out of lives, the game-over screen shows.
  4. Winning. Collect every coin and the win screen shows while everything freezes.
  5. Play again. A new game restores all the coins and pellets, resets your lives to 3, and starts over. Coins and pellets are hidden rather than destroyed, so nothing needs to be rebuilt.

A single game manager object handles all of this and persists across scenes.

Graph and colliders

  • Every waypoint has a neighbor list filled in by hand. In the Scene view, red gizmo lines draw those connections.
  • The player has a Rigidbody and a child sphere tagged PacMan_Sphere.
  • Coins, pellets and ghosts use trigger colliders.

Tunnels and collectibles

  • A teleport trigger on each tunnel entrance warps whatever enters (Pac-Man or a ghost) to the opposite side. Ghosts keep their pathing from the new spot.
  • Coins count toward the win and play a chomp sound. Power pellets scare all four ghosts.
